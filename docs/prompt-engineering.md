# Prompt Engineering Guide

## Getting the Most Out of Imagen 3

### Basic Principles

1. **Be Specific and Detailed**
   - Include clear subject matter
   - Specify desired style
   - Mention lighting and atmosphere
   - Include color preferences

2. **Use Technical Terms**
   - Camera settings (e.g., "shot with 85mm lens")
   - Lighting conditions (e.g., "golden hour lighting")
   - Artistic styles (e.g., "impressionist style")
   - Post-processing effects (e.g., "subtle HDR")

### Advanced Techniques

1. **Style Combinations**
   - Mix different artistic movements
   - Combine multiple techniques
   - Layer different effects
   - Blend time periods

2. **Atmosphere Control**
   - Time of day settings
   - Weather conditions
   - Seasonal effects
   - Mood indicators

### Example Prompts

1. **Nature Scene:**
   ```
   A serene mountain lake at sunrise, shot with a wide-angle lens. 
   Mist rising from the water, golden hour lighting, and autumn colors. 
   Photorealistic style with subtle HDR enhancement.
   ```

2. **Urban Scene:**
   ```
   Futuristic cityscape at night, neon lights reflecting in rain-slicked streets. 
   Cyberpunk aesthetic, shot from a low angle with a 35mm lens. 
   Deep contrast and vibrant colors.
   ```

### Tips for Better Results

1. **Resolution and Quality**
   - Specify desired resolution
   - Request high-quality details
   - Consider aspect ratio
   - Mention detail levels

2. **Color and Lighting**
   - Use specific color schemes
   - Define lighting direction
   - Mention time of day
   - Include atmosphere effects

3. **Common Issues**
   - Avoid vague descriptions
   - Don't overload with details
   - Balance technical and creative elements
   - Test and iterate prompts
